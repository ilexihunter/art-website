Twenty-Four

Twenty-Four is a series of images displaying a fragment of a narrative. Shot in a cinematic style (mise en scène), each image contains minimal information, allowing the viewer to continue the story for themselves. “Twenty Four” is a reference to the number of frames per second in cinema and the amount of exposures on a standard roll of film. Photography and cinema are closely related, and this work explores the synergy between the two.
