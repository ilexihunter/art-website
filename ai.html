AI

AI: the buzzword we all know and love (hate?) as of the past few years. For me, learning about AI has been an unlock to my career in marketing and has springboarded my passion for solving problems in smart ways.

A little over a year ago, a role in AI didn't exist on the marketing team at Guild. I kept running into problems I knew I could solve using AI, so I researched, built tools, and found community with like-minded people trying to do the same thing. Today, I'm Guild Education's Storytelling Manager & Marketing AI Enablement Lead, the first person doing AI enablement work for marketing here. Here's what led there, and what came out of it.

Sept 2025

Promoted to Storytelling Manager

Took over Guild's storytelling function: interviewing learners and surfacing stories used across 100+ employer partners, including Disney, Chipotle, PepsiCo, Discover, and JPMorgan Chase.

Storytelling Librarian

The storytelling team was spending hours searching a 400+ PDF content repository with a vendor tool that cost $50,000 a year. I built an internal AI agent that replaced it for free, originally as a full Glean agent and later rebuilt as a Claude skill, which is what the team uses today.

$50K saved annually · 400+ PDFs searchable in seconds
C A colleague, after her company-wide presentation on Storytelling Librarian
"It doesn't even feel like she's presenting. It feels like she's just talking, and I'm captivated."

Guild Academy

Selected for Guild Academy, the company's internal AI product, and interviewed by Guild's internal team to help explain the company's AI approach to others, work that also helps sell Academy to external buyers.

The Commercial Plugin

Designed and built a skill-based AI infrastructure for Guild's corporate marketing and commercial teams, giving people a faster, click-based way to find information and build their own workflows.

150+ users adopted it

AI Workbench

Built a resource that breaks down AI terms and concepts in plain language, so marketing teammates know where to start and feel confident experimenting.

Built the Learner Dashboard

Built an AI-powered dashboard, in Replit, that pulls stats from learner stories into one place, so the storytelling team and the rest of the company can see and share what's happening with members faster.

A Workflow Automation That Sparked a Chain Reaction

Built a Zapier automation that takes over the moment a learner books a call: sends a follow-up, a DocuSign, and updates the calendar invite automatically. It replaced a manual process that had been in place for 5+ years. Someone on the Recruiting team saw it and built their own version for employment docs the same week.

Training Every Subteam in Marketing

Held hands-on AI trainings for each marketing subteam, helping people scope their actual problems and figure out which AI approach, if any, made sense.
