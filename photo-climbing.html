Climbing Documentary

One day, when I was at the local climbing gym, I overheard some other climbers talking about their upcoming trip. I had a surge of confidence (or nervous spontaneity?) and I asked them if I could join. They said yes, and of course, I brought my camera. There is a certain level of purpose and focus that coincides with the art of climbing. Here you see that exploration of the intentionality and adventure of the sport immortalized in photographic image.
