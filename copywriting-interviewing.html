Interviewing & Storytelling

As Guild’s Storytelling Manager, I interview learners across our employer partners and turn their experiences into stories used in internal comms, sales materials, and company-wide features. A sample below, published for Black History Month, plus a few individual learner profiles.

Learner Voices — Black History Month

Published February 2024. Three Guild learners on going back to school later in their careers.

“For the first time in my life, I made Dean’s honor roll…I was on top of the world.”

John W., PepsiCo — thirty years with the company, first-generation degree earner

“So that’s the next step just to really evolve into another role where I can inspire others through my journey.”

Verna W., PNC — pursuing a People & Business Leadership Certificate

“It feels to me like the company is giving each of us an opportunity to make ourselves better while we are here.”

Matthew C., Gentiva — AWS Cloud Practitioner certificate, cybersecurity track

Rocket — Learner Voices

A separate round of interviews with Rocket employees pursuing degrees and certificates through Guild.

“Let’s be grateful for the help that we get. But even when it’s hard, remind yourself. You can do hard things. I’m living proof.”

Tricia J., Rocket

“We’re improving our ability to communicate, we’re improving our ability to collaborate, and we’re bringing new perspectives and new ideas into the business every day because of it.”

Ryan T., Rocket — pursuing two master’s degrees through Rock Academy
