Hello!

My name is Lexi Hunter, and I'm thrilled you're here! I'm an artist with a passion for creative entertainment, specializing in writing, photography, filmmaking, and content strategy/design. I'm originally from Nashville, Tennessee (so you can bet I have a great buttermilk biscuit recipe), and during that chapter of my life, I was surrounded by rich sounds and a vibrant culture that sparked my love for both storytelling and visual arts.

In 2018, right after graduating with a photo/film degree from Watkins College, I decided to do something crazy and move across the country and work at Disneyland. This adventure kindled a love for writing and inspired me to pursue a master's degree in professional creative writing from the University of Denver. One of the highlights of my journey was discovering Guild Education, where I worked as a senior storyteller and copywriter. Over the years, I've gained more than 5 years of marketing experience, ranging from content creation/design to copywriting and brand voice.

In addition to my degrees and work experience, I'm proud to hold a certificate in women's leadership from Yale and a certificate in digital marketing from eCornell, which both have deeply enhanced my career journey. Now, I'm excitedly exploring new opportunities where I can bring my creative vision and storytelling expertise to new projects — including hands-on work with AI tools.

Thank you for visiting my site and taking the time to learn about me. I can't wait to see how our stories may intersect!
